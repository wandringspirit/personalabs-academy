LESSON 2.1 ⏱ 25 Mins • Core Tech

The 2-Layer Face-Lock Pipeline (IP-Adapter + InsightFace Swap)

Relying on text prompts alone gives you only ~70% facial similarity. Relying on a cheap face-swap app alone gives you a flat "pasted-on" face with wrong head shapes. The secret is our 2-Layer Hybrid Lock:

  • Layer 1 (During Generation — IP-Adapter FaceID Plus v2): Inside SeaArt/Tensor ControlNet settings, upload your 00_MASTER_ANCHOR image into Reference / IP-Adapter FaceID. Set Weight to 0.75. This forces the AI to generate the exact skull shape, hair hairline, and eye spacing of your character.
  • Layer 2 (Post-Generation Polish — InsightFace / FaceSwap): Run the generated photo through SeaArt's built-in Face Swapper (or Discord InsightFace `Picsi.Ai`) using the same 00_MASTER_ANCHOR photo. This locks 100% of the micro-features (beauty mark, nose bridge, lip shape).
Why This Beats Custom LoRA Training on Day 1 Training a custom Kohya_ss LoRA requires 25 consistent photos first. Use the 2-Layer Hybrid Lock to generate your first 25 consistent angles in 30 minutes, and you can optionally one-click train a permanent Flux LoRA inside Tensor.art later!

LESSON 3.4 ⏱ 15 Mins • Post-Processing

The CapCut Mobile "Anti-AI" Color Grade & 4-Second Loop Formula

Never upload raw video straight from Kling AI to Instagram. Raw AI video has metadata tags and slightly soft edges. Run this 90-second CapCut Mobile workflow on every reel:

  • 1. Trim to 4.2 – 5.5 Seconds: Short reels loop automatically while people read your caption—skyrocketing your Average Percentage Viewed (APV) above 140%!
  • 2. Apply the "Anti-AI" Crispness Adjustment: In CapCut → Adjust → set Sharpen +18, Grain +8, Contrast +5, Saturation -4. That tiny bit of film grain masks AI smoothness and makes it look like an iPhone 15 night shot.
  • 3. Export Settings: Export at 1080p, 60fps, High Bitrate. This strips the original AI generator metadata and creates a fresh `.MOV` / `.MP4` container.
